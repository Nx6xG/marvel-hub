Proof that Tony Stark has a heart
Status: On the lake — as a wreath
The story

First a life-saver (kept the shrapnel from Tony's heart), then the power source of every suit, finally a funeral offering: 'Proof that Tony Stark has a heart' floated across the lake. Howard conceived the technology; Tony built it in a cave. With a box of scraps.
