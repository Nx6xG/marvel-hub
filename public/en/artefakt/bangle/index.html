Kamalas Bangle

A quantum band with family history
Status: On Kamala's wrist
The story

The family heirloom that awakened Kamala's powers — actually a quantum band with an unknown counterpart. It tore holes in space, swapped three heroines across the universe, and is waiting for someone to find the second band. Another thread pointing at the future.
