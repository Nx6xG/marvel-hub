Cerebro

Xavier's window to the world
Status: Intact, depending on the timeline
The story

The amplifier that lets Xavier find every mutant on Earth — and, as X2 proved, potentially paralyze every human: Cerebro is a tracking device AND a weapon of mass destruction, depending on who wears the helmet. On Earth-838, Professor X used it to his final second.

  • 2003: Stryker's Dark Cerebro attack
  • 838: the Illuminati version
