The Book of the Damned
Status: All copies destroyed — the knowledge is immortal
The story

Chthon's compendium of dark magic that corrupts every reader. It turned Wanda's grief into a multiversal rampage. She destroyed every copy in every universe — but Agatha had already proven how many paths dark knowledge finds.
