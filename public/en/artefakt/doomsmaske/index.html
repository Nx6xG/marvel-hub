Dooms Maske

The face of Latveria
Status: In theaters December 18, 2026
The story

In the comics it hides a disfigured face — or a single scar that Doom's vanity cannot bear; the legend changes with the writer. In the MCU it carries the face of Robert Downey Jr., and that is precisely the point: 'New mask, same task.' What lies beneath is Doomsday's best-kept secret.
