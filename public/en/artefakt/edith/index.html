Even Dead, I'm The Hero

The story

Tony's parting gift to Peter: glasses with access to Stark's entire defense network, drone fleet included. Peter gave them to Mysterio within five minutes — the mistake that led to his unmasking and thus to No Way Home. Where E.D.I.T.H. is since the great forgetting, no one has answered.
