Der Infinity Gauntlet

Two gauntlets, two snaps
Status: Both destroyed
The story

Eitri forged the first on Nidavellir — under duress, as the last of his people. Tony's nano version was Earth's answer. Both gauntlets united all six stones, and both took their price: Thanos' arm, Hulk's arm, Tony's life. No object in the MCU has cost more.

Milestones
  • 2018: forged on Nidavellir
  • 2018: the Snap
  • 2023: Hulk's Blip snap
  • 2023: 'I am Iron Man' — the last snap
