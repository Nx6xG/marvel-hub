Das Herzkraut

Wakanda's kingmaker
Status: Burned — synthetically recreated
The story

The vibranium-infused plant that grants the Black Panthers their power and leads them to the Ancestral Plane. Killmonger burned the reserves; Shuri recreated the herb synthetically — and met him, of all people, on the Ancestral Plane. Talokan, by the way, has its own version of this story.

Milestones
  • Prehistory: grows on vibranium soil
  • 2018: burned by Killmonger
  • 2025: Shuri's synthesis
  • Today: Wakanda's secret, again
