Vibranium & Adamantium

The two wonder metals
Status: Vibranium: Wakanda/Talokan · Adamantium: being mined
The story

Vibranium absorbs energy (Wakanda's meteorite, Cap's shield, Talokan's reefs) — adamantium is virtually indestructible (Wolverine's skeleton, the Fox legacy). Brave New World officially brought adamantium into the MCU: harvested from the dead Celestial in the Indian Ocean. A metal with X-Men written all over it.

Milestones
  • Prehistory: the vibranium meteorite hits Wakanda
  • 1945: Cap's shield sinks into the ice
  • 2021: the Celestial freezes
  • 2025: adamantium mining begins — the race is on
