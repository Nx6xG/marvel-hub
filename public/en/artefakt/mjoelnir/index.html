Status: Destroyed (2017) — the 2013 version rests in New Asgard
The story

Forged in the heart of a dying star, with a built-in worthiness test. Hela crushed it with one hand; Cap proved in Endgame what everyone suspected since Age of Ultron — the elevator moment of cinema history. The version borrowed from 2013 stayed with Jane and now rests in New Asgard.
