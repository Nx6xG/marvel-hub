Die Necroschwert-Klinge

All-Black, the god-slayer
Status: Destroyed — along with Gorr's revenge
The story

The first weapon of the symbiote god Knull (in the comics) — in the MCU, the blade that found a grieving Gorr and made him the God Butcher. It whispers, corrupts, and in the end opened the gate to Eternity. Destroyed — but Venom 3 teased Knull. Symbiote threads never end cleanly.
