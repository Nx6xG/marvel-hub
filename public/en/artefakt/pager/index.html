Furys Pager

The retro device that paged hope
Status: Purpose served
The story

A 90s pager with a Kree upgrade and exactly one function: call Carol. Fury carried it for 23 years and pressed the button only as he crumbled to dust — perhaps the best prop punchline in the MCU: the entire Infinity War post-credit scene was this device.

Milestones
  • 1995: Carol's parting gift
  • 2018: the last button press
  • 2019: 'Where's Fury?'
