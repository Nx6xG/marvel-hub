Captain Americas Schild

Vibranium, a proto-adamantium legacy
Status: With Sam Wilson
The story

Howard Stark's vibranium masterpiece and the symbol of three generations: Steve carried it, dropped it after Civil War, and handed it to Sam as an old man. In between, John Walker defiled it with blood — perhaps the strongest single scene of the streaming era.

Milestones
  • 1943: built by Howard Stark
  • 2016: left behind in Siberia
  • 2023: handed to Sam
  • 2024: Walker's bloodstain
  • Today: Sam Wilson, Captain America
