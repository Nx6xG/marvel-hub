Die Infinity-Steine

Six singularities
Status: Destroyed (2018 & 2023) — variants exist across the multiverse
The story

Six remnants of creation, each an aspect of existence. Thanos needed them for the Snap, the Avengers for the Blip — then Thanos destroyed them in the past, and the borrowed duplicates were returned. The TVA, by the way, keeps whole drawers full of them. As paperweights.

The six stones
Space Stone · TesseractFrom Hydra weapon to S.H.I.E.L.D. lab to Loki's 2012 theft — the best-travelled object in the MCU.Mind Stone · the ScepterFrom Loki via Hydra to Ultron — and, as Vision's forehead jewel, finally a living being.Reality Stone · the AetherA liquid parasite inside Jane Foster, then left with the Collector — the strangest of the six.Power Stone · the OrbFound on Morag, guarded by Xandar, claimed first by Thanos.Time Stone · Eye of AgamottoGuarded by Kamar-Taj, used 14 million times by Strange — Thanos got it anyway.Soul Stone · VormirDemands a sacrifice: Gamora fell for Thanos, Natasha for the Avengers.
