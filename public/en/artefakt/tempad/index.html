TVA-Technik

TemPads, prune sticks, Miss Minutes
Status: In constant use
The story

The agency at the end of time runs on retro tech that mocks all physics: TemPads open doors through space and time, prune sticks erase things from existence (well — into the Void), and Miss Minutes is an AI with a southern accent and her own agenda. Deadpool stole a TemPad. Of course he did.
