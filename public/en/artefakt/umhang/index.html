Der Cloak of Levitation

The most loyal supporting actor
Status: With Strange
The story

A relic with a will of its own and strong opinions: it chose Strange in the Sanctum, wipes away his tears and yanks him out of the line of fire. Officially a cloak, in practice a pet.
