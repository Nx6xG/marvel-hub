Das Book of Vishanti

The good counterpart to the Darkhold
Status: Destroyed — allegedly
The story

The weapon against all darkness: it gives a sorcerer exactly the spell they need. Strange and America reached it after a multiverse marathon — and Wanda destroyed it seconds later. Whether the original book is truly gone, the film deliberately left open.
