Web-Shooter & Spinnennetz

A chemistry genius on your wrist
Status: Home-built, 2026 edition
The story

The eternal nerd debate: Maguire's Peter produces webbing organically, Holland and Garfield build shooters themselves — and Peter's web formula is canonically one of New York's most valuable inventions that nobody pays him a cent for. After the forgetting spell, Holland's Peter rebuilt everything: suit, shooters, life.
