Yondus Yaka-Pfeil

A whistle is enough
Status: With Kraglin
The story

An arrow of Yaka metal, steered by whistling alone — in Yondu's hands a one-man army: the corridor sequence in Vol. 2 is one of the most iconic scenes in the MCU. After his sacrifice, Kraglin inherited arrow and fin, and needed two movies to learn the whistle.
