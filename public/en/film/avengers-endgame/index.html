The story

After the devastating events of Avengers: Infinity War, the universe is in ruins due to the efforts of the Mad Titan, Thanos. With the help of remaining allies, the Avengers must assemble once more in order to undo Thanos' actions and restore order to the universe once and for all, no matter what consequences may be in store.

Trivia & background
  • At $2.799 billion, for a time the most successful film of all time.
  • 'I am Iron Man' as the last line was a late reshoot idea — in the script, Tony initially died without a word.
🥚 Easter eggs & references
  • Steve as 'America's ass' — confirmed by himself in the time-heist fight against 2012 Cap.
  • The teenager at Tony's funeral is Harley from Iron Man 3 — six years older.
  • 'Hail Hydra': Steve weaponizes the 2016 comics twist as an elevator trick — fan service as a punchline.
  • Howard the Duck stands on the battlefield in the portals finale. Really.
📍 Filming locations
  • The 2012 New York time heist used sets and archive footage — the compound finale was created entirely in Atlanta.
  • Tony's lakeside cabin: Bouckaert Farm in Georgia — a popular fan day trip today.
