The story

The story follows Carol Danvers as she becomes one of the universe’s most powerful heroes when Earth is caught in the middle of a galactic war between two alien races. Set in the 1990s, Captain Marvel is an all-new adventure from a previously unseen period in the history of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Trivia & background
  • Samuel L. Jackson was digitally de-aged by 25 years for the entire film — a first at the time.
  • Goose isn't a cat but a Flerken. That explains the Tesseract in the stomach.
🥚 Easter eggs & references
  • The Stan Lee fanfare: every logo built from his cameos — the first film after his death.
  • The pager retroactively explains Fury's Infinity War stinger — and Goose explains the eyepatch.
📍 Filming locations
  • The 90s Blockbuster backdrop: Los Angeles, including rebuilt Blockbuster stores and Edwards Air Force Base.
