The story

A listless Wade Wilson toils away in civilian life with his days as the morally flexible mercenary, Deadpool, behind him. But when his homeworld faces an existential threat, Wade must reluctantly suit-up again with an even more reluctant Wolverine.

  • Most successful R-rated film of all time ($1.34 billion).

  • The Void is a Fox graveyard: the 20th Century logo in ruins, Alioth from Loki, cameos from Elektra to Blade.
  • Wolverine's yellow suit, demanded for 24 years, finally appears — Honda Odyssey included.
  • Nicepool's world nods deep — Dogpool comes from a real comics variant.
