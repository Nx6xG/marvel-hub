Trivia & background
  • Kurt Russell was fully digitally de-aged for the opening — and the Baby Groot dance is based on James Gunn's own dance video.
🥚 Easter eggs & references
  • The five post-credit scenes contain the original 1969 Guardians — Stallone's Ravager crew is the 60s team.
  • Baby Groot's opening dance relegates an entire boss fight to background action.
📍 Filming locations
  • Ego's planet grew at Pinewood Atlanta Studios (now Trilith) — the heart of almost every later MCU shoot.
