The story

After stealing the Tesseract during the events of “Avengers: Endgame,” an alternate version of Loki is brought to the mysterious Time Variance Authority, a bureaucratic organization that exists outside of time and space and monitors the timeline. They give Loki a choice: face being erased from existence due to being a “time variant” or help fix the timeline and stop a greater threat.

Trivia & background
  • The TVA aesthetic is based on 70s bureaucracy design; Miss Minutes was voiced live on set by an actress.
  • Kang's casting as He Who Remains was the original cornerstone of the whole saga — until the Doom pivot came.
🥚 Easter eggs & references
  • In the TVA archive: Mjölnir as a paperweight and a drawer full of Infinity Stones — 'Some of the guys use them as paperweights.'
  • The Void landscape: the Thanos-copter, Throg in a jar, a Living Tribunal head, Yellowjacket's helmet.
  • Episode 1 reveals Loki as D. B. Cooper — his explanation for America's most famous unsolved crime.
📍 Filming locations
  • The TVA is a brutalist office dream from Atlanta — including the Marriott Marquis lobby as a set.
