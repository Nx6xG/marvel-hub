The story

Against the vibrant backdrop of a 1960s-inspired, retro-futuristic world, Marvel's First Family is forced to balance their roles as heroes with the strength of their family bond, while defending Earth from a ravenous space god called Galactus and his enigmatic Herald, Silver Surfer.

Trivia & background
  • Set entirely on Earth-828 — named after Jack Kirby's birthday (August 28).
  • After the cloud debacle of 2007, Galactus was shown fully physical for the first time (Ralph Ineson).
🥚 Easter eggs & references
  • Earth-828: the number honors Jack Kirby's birthday; the opening imitates 60s newsreels.
  • H.E.R.B.I.E. comes from the 1978 cartoon — invented back then as a Human Torch substitute.
  • The animated credits quote the 1967 Hanna-Barbera F4 series.
📍 Filming locations
  • The retro future of Earth-828 was built at Pinewood Studios London — deliberately with 60s studio-craft aesthetics.
