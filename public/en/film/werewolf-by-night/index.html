The story

Monster hunters compete in black and white for the Bloodstone — Marvel's horror corner as an homage to Universal classics. Composer Michael Giacchino's directing debut.

Cast

Gael García Bernal · Laura Donnelly

Trivia & background
  • Shot entirely in black and white as an homage to Universal horror — composer Michael Giacchino directed for the first time.
🥚 Easter eggs & references
  • The film scratches, cue marks and mono sound imitate 1930s Universal horror in detail.
  • Man-Thing is called Ted. Whoever knows fear burns at his touch — a comics rule casually demonstrated.
