Knowhere

The head of the dead Celestial
The story

A mining station inside the severed head of a dead Celestial — black market, refuge and seat of the Collector, who archives everything remarkable in the universe there. Taken over by the Guardians after Thanos' attack and their home base today. Yes: this website is named after it.
