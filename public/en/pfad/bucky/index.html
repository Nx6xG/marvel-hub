Bucky's 80 Years

From best friend to weapon to redemption

No MCU character has a longer arc: in 1943 Bucky falls from the train — and in 2026 stands against Doom as a New Avenger. In between: brainwashing, guilt and the slowest healing process in the franchise.

The path · 6 steps
1
Captain America: The First Avenger
The best friend: Bucky falls — seemingly to his death.
2
Captain America: The Winter Soldier
The twist: Hydra's Winter Soldier is Bucky. 'Who the hell is Bucky?'
3
Captain America: Civil War
The tape: Zemo activates him, the Avengers break over him.
4
The Falcon and the Winter Soldier
The therapy: amends list, Wakandan acquittal, peace with himself.
5
Thunderbolts*
The new family: congressman, then New Avenger.
6
Avengers: Doomsday
The proving ground: 80 years of experience against Doom.
