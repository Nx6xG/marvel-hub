Oddities & box-office records
  • Longest film: Avengers: Endgame (3 h 1 min) — shortest: Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer (1 h 32 min).
  • Avengers: Endgame grossed $2.799 billion and was, for a time, the most successful film of all time.
  • Deadpool & Wolverine is the most successful R-rated film in history at $1.34 billion.
  • No Way Home ($1.9 billion) is the most successful Spider-Man film — and the biggest pandemic-era opening.
  • Morbius is the only film that flopped twice: Sony re-released it because of the memes.
  • Iron Man was financed with mortgaged character rights — had it flopped, this wiki would not exist.
  • Guardians Vol. 2 holds the credits record with 5 post-credit scenes.
  • X-Men '97 and Ms. Marvel (98% each) beat every theatrical film.
  • Secret Invasion cost ~$212 million for 6 episodes — at 53% RT.
  • Loki is the only MCU series that ever got a second season.
  • Howard the Duck (1986) was the very first Marvel theatrical film. Produced by George Lucas. 13%.
