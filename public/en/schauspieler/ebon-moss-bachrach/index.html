Biography

Ebon Che Moss-Bachrach (/ˈɛbɪn mɑːsˈbækˈræk/) (born March 19, 1977) is an American actor. He is known for his role as restaurant manager Richie Jerimovich in the comedy-drama series The Bear (2022–2026), for which he was twice awarded the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Comedy Series, in addition to two Golden Globe Award nominations. Moss-Bachrach has also had major television roles on Girls (2014–2017) and NOS4A2 (2019–2020), and appeared in the first season of Andor (2022).
