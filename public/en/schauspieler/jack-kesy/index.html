Biography

Jack Kesy is an American actor. On television, he has portrayed Gabriel Bolivar on the FX horror drama The Strain (2014–2016) and Roller Husser on the TNT dark comedy Claws (2017–present). He also played Black Tom Cassidy in the Marvel Comics film Deadpool 2 (2018).
