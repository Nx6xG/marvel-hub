Biography

Ralph Michael Ineson (/ˈaɪnsən/; born 15 December 1969) is an English actor and narrator. Standing 6 feet 3 inches tall with a distinctively deep voice and Yorkshire accent, he often plays authority figures. Ineson's notable film roles include Amycus Carrow in the final three Harry Potter films (2009–2011), William in The Witch (2015), the title character in The Green Knight (2021), Dr Wilhelm Sievers in Nosferatu (2024), and Galactus in The Fantastic Four: First Steps (2025). His notable television roles include Chris Finch in The Office (2001), Dagmer Cleftjaw in Game of Thrones (2012), and Nikolai Tarakanov in Chernobyl (2019).
