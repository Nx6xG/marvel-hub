Biography

Born in London, England, Robin Atkin Downes is an English actor who is one of the most prolific voice-over actors in Los Angeles. He is well known for his work in film, television and voice acting. He is best known for playing Atoq Navarro and Tenzin in the Uncharted series, Kazuhira "Kaz" Miller in Metal Gear Solid: Peace Walker, the Prophet of Regret in Halo 2 and Halo Wars, Travis Touchdown, the main protagonist in the No More Heroes series, Minh Young Kim, Kantus and Chaps in Gears of War and Gears of War 2, The Medic in Team Fortress 2, Luxord in Kingdom Hearts II, and hundreds of other games.
