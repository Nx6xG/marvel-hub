Biography

Shelby Young (born April 8, 1992) is an American actress. Her latest films include Columbia Picture's The Social Network and Universal's Wild Child. She also voices all of the Lucky Charms, Trix and Coco Puffs commercials, and play the role of Kinsey on Days of our Lives.
