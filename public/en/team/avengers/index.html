Status: Disbanded — no official roster since Endgame
The story

Nick Fury's answer to a world full of threats: six egos, one team. They won New York, lost to Thanos, brought the universe back — and fell apart afterwards. That there has been no standing Avengers team for years is exactly the gap Doom is stepping into: Doomsday has to answer who inherits the name.
