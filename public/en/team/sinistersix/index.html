The story

The team that never quite existed: Sony planned the Sinister Six since the Amazing era; No Way Home at least gathered five multiverse villains (with Sandman and Lizard) against three Spider-Men. Mysterio and Vulture remain eternal candidates. Green Goblin meanwhile proved one man with bombs is enough to destroy everything.
