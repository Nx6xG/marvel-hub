Celestials

The cosmic titans
The story

Cosmic titans who seed galaxies: their embryos gestate inside planetary cores and consume the world at birth. Ego was one, Tiamut was stopped by the Eternals — his frozen body is an adamantium mine today, and Knowhere sits in the skull of a third.
