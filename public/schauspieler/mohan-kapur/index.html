Biografie

Mohan Kapur (born October 27, 1965) is an Indian actor and television host. Kapur has appeared in the popular Bollywood films Jolly LLB, Happy New Year, and Mission Mangal. Kapur is also known for his work as a voice artist and has dubbed for numerous Hollywood actors including Dwayne Johnson, Tom Hardy, Nicolas Cage and Jackie Chan. In 2021, he was cast as Yusuf Khan in the MCU titles Ms. Marvel (2022), The Marvels (2023), and Daredevil: Born Again (2025).
