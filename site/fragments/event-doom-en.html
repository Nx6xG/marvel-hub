August 14, 2026 · D23

A new trailer — and Marvel shows its hand

A new Doomsday trailer ran at the Disney showcase: Doom raises a Sentinel army, Sue Storm warns the heroes about him, Reed Richards confronts him — and Thor attacks with Stormbreaker. On stage: Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans and Hayley Atwell; Hugh Jackman appeared in a video message, Ryan Reynolds from off-screen. Even so, there was no confirmation for Deadpool and Wolverine. Also on display: a second Doom costume plus the suits of Nightcrawler and Mystique.

August 14, 2026 · D23

After the saga: the X-Men reboot gets a date

On May 5, 2028 the X-Men reboot opens Phase 7 — with Sadie Sink as Jean Grey, Kit Connor as Cyclops, Christopher Abbott as Professor X, Samara Weaving as Emma Frost, Inde Navarrette as Rogue, Maya Boyd as Storm and Adam Driver as Mister Sinister. Jake Schreier (Thunderbolts*) directs. Which means: the world after Secret Wars already has a schedule.

July 2026 · Comic-Con

Hall H completely loses it

An exclusive in-room trailer: Doom stops Thor's Stormbreaker with his bare hand and awakens a Sentinel army. Also confirmed: Ryan Gosling plays Ghost Rider, David Jonsson is the new Black Panther.
