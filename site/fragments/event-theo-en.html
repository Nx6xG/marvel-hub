The hottest fan theories with an editorial plausibility rating — based on comics, cast list and leaks.

After Secret Wars: a new, unified timeline with mutants

The saga's meta purpose: Secret Wars as an elegant reset that merges the Fox legacy, Sony contracts and 17 years of continuity into a fresh timeline — with mutants who were "always there". After that: an X-Men reboot. Practically every insider source points this way.

90 %

Doomsday ends with the birth of Battleworld

Comic template

The obvious two-parter structure after Hickman: the heroes lose, the incursions win — and the cliffhanger is Doom's stitched-together world, on which Secret Wars then takes place. Infinity War proved the Russos have the courage for a villain victory.

88 %

Franklin Richards is Doom's power source

Comic template

In the comics, Franklin's universe-creating power feeds Battleworld. The MCU has paved the way: Doom stands next to the boy in the F4 post-credit scene. The film's emotional engine could simply be: Reed and Sue want their child back — from a god.

82 %

Loki's timeline tree is the real target

Loki physically holds all timelines together. Whoever wants to control the multiverse or let it collapse must go through him. Either Doom breaks the tree — or Loki has to make the hardest decision of the saga: let branches die to save the trunk. Otherwise Hiddleston's casting makes little sense.

78 %

Hugh Jackman shows up (again)

Not officially in the cast — but his Wolverine survived Deadpool & Wolverine, his universe hangs on him as its anchor being, and Secret Wars without the best X-Man would be a strange farewell party. At D23 2026 Jackman appeared in a video message with Ryan Reynolds heckling from off-screen — and still nothing was confirmed. Marvel is audibly playing with the expectation.

72 %

The Council of Kangs gets wiped out by Doom

Comic logic

Elegant for Marvel: the open Kang thread is used for characterization — Doom eliminates the Council in one sequence and proves what league he plays in. The SDCC footage (Doom stops Stormbreaker with one hand) hints at exactly this power demonstration.

68 %

Chris Evans is NOT playing Steve Rogers

Evans is confirmed, his role is not. He waved off Steve comebacks for years — but gleefully played Johnny Storm in 2024. A Human Torch variant or a dark Steve from another universe would be the more interesting road; old-man Steve as an emotional coda remains conceivable.

60 %

The dead return as variants

The multiverse makes every farewell negotiable: an Iron Man variant, a Black Widow, a T'Challa from another world. Marvel will have to use this sparingly or it devalues Endgame — but one or two targeted appearances are considered certain. The new Black Panther could be exactly such a variant.

58 %

Doom is a Tony Stark variant

Hotly debated

Why else RDJ? The theory: in another universe, Tony didn't become Iron Man but Doom — a Tony who could never stop "saving the world". Against it: the Russos stress that Doom is a completely separate character. For it: Marvel doesn't cast RDJ without a reason.

55 %

Mephisto is the puppet master behind Doom

Since Ironheart, Mephisto is real. The theory: Doom's magic has a price, and the devil collects in the end. Currently more wishful thinking than evidence — in the comics, Doom never needed to play second fiddle to anyone.
